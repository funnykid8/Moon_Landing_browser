Data & method

Positions use Meeus/ELP truncated lunar & solar theory, with libration, from the system clock (no network needed). Accuracy ≈ 0.1–0.2°, so use it for comparing sites and planning; cross‑check final numbers with NASA JPL Horizons or SPICE, and terrain with LRO LOLA data. Flat‑horizon model; the mask slider approximates local terrain.
